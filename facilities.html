A Distributed Laboratory

Integrate, don't duplicate.

CRESST establishes the region's missing electronics-specific failure-analysis front end while integrating UCF's advanced materials-characterization infrastructure and NeoCity's advanced-packaging and acoustic-microscopy capability rather than rebuilding either.

01 Radiation exposure Proton, heavy-ion, or pulsed-laser campaign under mission-relevant conditions.
02 Failure capture Electrical fault verification and curve tracing while the signature is still fresh.
03 Nondestructive inspection X-ray and micro-CT of package, die attach, bonds, and interconnect before the part is opened.
04 Fault localization Thermal, IR, and photon-emission imaging plus microprobing to find the site.
05 Root-cause determination Controlled decapsulation, cross-sectioning, and physical root cause.
06 Redesign & re-test Findings feed derating, screening, and part selection — then the loop runs again.

Closed loop — findings from step 06 re-enter at step 01

Borrow, Not Buy

Instruments CRESST will not purchase.

Rather than duplicating UCF's high-end materials instruments, the project reserves $100,000 over two years for priority access to and technical support from the UCF Materials Characterization Facility.
