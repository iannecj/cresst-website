University of Central Florida

Qualification capability for the electronics space missions depend on.

CRESST, UCF's Center for Reliability Evaluation of Semiconductor and Space Technologies, develops agile assurance strategies for commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) and hybrid architectures, connecting part selection, radiation and reliability testing, advanced packaging, and failure analysis into a single qualification pipeline. Through ARCTIC (Artemis-Ready Cryogenic Technologies for Instrumentation and Power Conversion), it also builds cold-capable power and instrument electronics for the lunar surface and deep space.

The Gap

Commercial space moves faster than traditional parts assurance can follow.

Commercial and government space programs increasingly fly COTS parts and hybrid architectures that mix COTS, automotive-grade, and space-qualified parts. Each part is chosen for performance, availability, and cost, and many fall outside the traditional parts classes, flight heritage, and lot-by-lot qualification that legacy assurance standards assume.

CRESST closes the gap with agile, environment-driven assurance strategies that build confidence in non-traditional parts for a specific use case and environment. It connects part selection, radiation and reliability testing, advanced packaging, and failure analysis into one qualification pipeline, built to replenish expertise and relieve test-infrastructure bottlenecks on a scale no other program can match.

Key Areas of Focus

Six disciplines. One qualification pipeline.

Every part CRESST touches moves through the same set of assurance disciplines, from first electrical stress test to a data package a program can act on.

01

Radiation & Environmental Assurance

Radiation effects, reliability, extreme temperature, and environmental stress testing for parts headed beyond low Earth orbit.

02

Electrical Stress & Power Systems

High-voltage, biased-stress, power-electronics, and application-relevant electrical testing, plus cold-capable space power hardware through ARCTIC.

03

Advanced Packaging & Heterogeneous Integration

Package-level reliability, materials, thermal/mechanical performance, and advanced integration strategies.

04

Failure Analysis & Materials Characterization

Root-cause analysis, a failure-signature library, and closed-loop assurance improvement.

05

COTS Parts Assurance & Data Packages

Part selection, screening, derating, alternate sourcing, and use-case-specific data packages.

06

Workforce Development & Education

Curriculum, certificate pathways, internships, mentorship, and continuous workforce replenishment.

Assurance Strategy

Confidence in non-traditional parts, matched to the mission.

  • Use-case driven — Assurance tailored to the application’s environment, lifetime, and consequence of failure, rather than a single parts class applied to every mission.
  • Hybrid and COTS architectures — Strategies for systems that mix COTS, automotive-grade, and space-qualified parts, including system-level mitigation where part-level heritage is missing.
  • Agile and data-driven — Fast-turn screening, radiation and environmental testing, and failure analysis that feed shared data, so programs can move at commercial schedules.
  • Florida’s semiconductor corridor — A complementary advanced-packaging partnership with the NSF-funded Florida Semiconductor Engine.
