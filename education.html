Education & Workforce

The Space U workforce pathway.

CRESST replenishes the space-electronics workforce through stewarded programs: credentialed coursework, hands-on radiation and failure-analysis work, and placement into the national supply chain.

Our goal is to replenish skills through stewarded workforce programs and build bottleneck-relieving infrastructure on a timetable, scale, and relevance no other program can match.

01 Recruit & Educate Engage students from diverse backgrounds and build foundational knowledge.
02 Train & Experience Hands-on learning with real hardware, NASA-aligned mentorship, and authentic projects.
03 Qualify & Certify Earn industry-recognized credentials and NASA-relevant competencies.
04 Deploy & Contribute Place talent into the space sector to strengthen the national workforce and mission success.
05 Sustain & Grow Reinvest, expand capabilities, and continuously replenish the workforce pipeline.
